Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Da$Nomad is a personal currency converter. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have.

Short version: Da$Nomad has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your preferences stay on your device. Location and weather requests go directly to the third-party services described below.

Information stored on your device

Da$Nomad stores your selected currencies, home location and timezone, cached exchange rates, and cached weather data in your device's local storage. This information is not sent to a Da$Nomad server. Removing the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Location

On startup, Da$Nomad may ask BigDataCloud for an approximate location based on your network request so it can select a nearby currency. This request includes your IP address, which is processed by BigDataCloud.

If you choose Use my current location, the app asks for device location permission and sends the coordinates to BigDataCloud for reverse geocoding. Coordinates may also be sent to Open-Meteo to show weather for your location. Da$Nomad does not store your coordinates on a server.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The rest of the app continues to work with your saved currencies.

Exchange rates and weather

Exchange rates are requested from ExchangeRate-API (Open ER API). Weather is requested from Open-Meteo. These requests do not contain your name, email address, or account information. As with any web request, the service providers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and request headers.

Third-party services

  • ExchangeRate-API privacy policy
  • BigDataCloud privacy policy
  • Open-Meteo privacy policy

What Da$Nomad does not do

  • It does not create an account or ask for your name or email address.
  • It does not include advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking.
  • It does not sell personal data or share it for advertising.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera.

Children

Da$Nomad is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
